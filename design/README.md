# GuideHunter design mockup

The visual reference for GuideHunter's screens, in BetterHunter's theme (its "field guide" palette and Barlow fonts),
which `core/designsystem` implements.

- **Live canvas (source of truth):** https://claude.ai/artifact/PCFgbxtcGyHHrSPPPRqKbL (a claude.ai Design canvas,
  private to its owner). Add new screens there, then copy the files back here.
- **Backup copy:** `mockup/`: the canvas index (`canvas.json`) and one `.dc.html` file per screen, as published.

Phone frames are 390×844; the tablet frame is 1280×800. Each page has a dark row on top and a light row below;
screens link to each other, so the flows can be clicked through on the canvas.

## Decisions shown in the mockup

- **Pins:** orange for hunting, teal for fishing, gold for both (older offers only: a new offer is one type); they differ in lightness too, not only in hue. The
  app draws the same pin (`feature/map/.../MapPins.kt`, colours in `core/designsystem` `PinColors`).
- **One type per offer:** step 1 offers Hunting or Fishing, with a note to make two offers for both; step 2 lists
  that type's species, alphabetically.
- **Dates:** a trip length (half day to weeks) and departures, one by one or weekly, these dates only or every year.
  The map's Dates chip asks when the user can go; an offer shows if a whole trip fits. The offer shows the next
  departure, the others under Other dates; the trip chosen goes above the contact and into the email.
- **Labels wrap:** a button's label goes on two lines rather than being cut (French labels are long).
- **The contact card** no longer hides the guide's contact behind grey bars: it says how to contact the guide (watch
  an ad, then send a request), and shows the guide's contact only once the request is accepted.
- **User and guide modes:** one account; at first launch, a welcome (sign in with Google, or Later) and "What brings
  you here?" choose the mode; Settings switches it.
- **Requests replace the contact reveal:** after the ad, "I'm interested" sends the user's name, email (locked to
  Google) and phone to the guide. Statuses: Pending, Accepted, Declined, Cancelled, Offer removed; the guide's contact
  shows once accepted.
- **Featured (boosted) offers:** a gold ring and star on the pin, drawn on top, the card first with a "Featured"
  badge; at most 3 at a time.
- **Native ads only in lists** (My requests, the guide's home, Requests received), labelled "Ad".
- **The pin without the map:** "Use my location" and "Type coordinates" under the map, for TalkBack and switch users
  (and a guide who knows the coordinates); the coordinates open inline, not in a dialog.
- **Tablets and unfolded foldables** (840 dp and wider): the guide's offer in a 400 dp pane beside the map, with a
  close button; the open guide's card is outlined in orange and its pin is larger.
- **The icon and splash: B, the pin.** The orange map pin with a crossed rifle and fishing rod in its head, in deep
  green: the pin says where, the crossing says hunting and fishing. Drawn bold (thick barrel and rod, simplified stock,
  scope and reel) so the crossing still reads at 48 px; Android 13's themed icon, in one colour, puts the crossing in
  a round window cut in the pin's head. `generator/logo.py` writes the app's icon drawables and this board's SVGs from
  one geometry: change the logo there. (A hook-pin variant was tried and dropped: the crossing was the point.)
- **Map:** the mockup draws a stylised southern Quebec. The app shows Google Maps (Compose), dark map style in dark
  theme.
- **Sample guides** are made up (`*.example` addresses); real ones come from Firestore.
